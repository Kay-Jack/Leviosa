Hall Effect Sensors:

Criteria to consider: 
- mT sensing range :
    - A larger number means a more sensitive sensor measured in mV/mT or mV/Guass, where 1 Guass is 0.1mT
    - reccomend this one for now (https://www.digikey.ca/en/products/detail/texas-instruments/DRV5053VAQDBZR/5015738) -90mV/mT


- Analog sensor:
    - Benefit of being fast in response 
    - only seems to be for one axis
    - Best candidate if multiple axises isnt needed 

- I2C device:
    - Slower than other options
    - programmable 
    - can read multiple axises

- Wheatstone Bridge:
    - Fast as analog 
    - single axis 
    - Few options to choose from
